I'm a Senior Solutions Architect at AWS in Worldwide Public Sector, where I work with education technology companies on generative AI, agentic systems, and ML platforms. I've worked in edtech for 19 years, at AWS since 2020.

I'm also a PhD candidate in Computer Science at National University (expected 2027). My dissertation tests whether prompt engineering patterns change the quality of LLM-generated code, measured by weighted defect density and cyclomatic complexity, and whether that effect is different for extended-thinking models. The dataset is 525 code samples across seven models, five prompt patterns, and fifteen Python scenarios.

Outside the day job I run [GenAI Under the Hood](https://www.youtube.com/@genaiunderthehood), a YouTube channel about running and testing LLMs on your own hardware. Most of my side projects start from the same hunch, that what you build around a model matters more than its size, and then try to measure it.

## Projects

| Project | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| [mine-harness](https://github.com/curtisdarst/mine-harness) | A Minesweeper testbed for local LLMs. Tiered tools take one job away from the model at a time, an exact solver grades every move against what was provable, and models can induce their own rules through a verifier gate. Runs on 8 GB of VRAM. |
| [sagemaker-modernbert-testing](https://github.com/curtisdarst/sagemaker-modernbert-testing) | Reproducible ModernBERT inference benchmarks on SageMaker AI. Deploys one model to CPU, GPU, Inferentia2, and Serverless endpoints and reports latency, throughput, batching, cold start, and cost per million inferences for nine configurations. |
| [research-triage-agent](https://github.com/curtisdarst/research-triage-agent) | A Google ADK research agent that checks every claim against its retrieved source and reports what it can't support. Ships with a reproducible citation-grounding eval. |
| [bedrock-nova-context-window](https://github.com/curtisdarst/bedrock-nova-context-window) | Tests the context window limits of Amazon Nova models on Bedrock. |
